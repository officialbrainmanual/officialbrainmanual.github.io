Privacy notice

Last updated 23 October 2026

Who we are

Official Brain Manual is a UK-based psychology and self-improvement brand. We are responsible for the details you give us on this page. To contact us about your data, reply to any email we send you.

What we collect

  • Your first and last name and email address, so we can send you the free guide.
  • Your mobile number, only if you choose to give it.
  • Whether you ticked the box to get tips and new guides, and where you found us (for example Instagram or X).

How we use it

  • To email you the free guide you asked for.
  • Only if you ticked the box: to send occasional psychology tips and tell you about new guides, some of them paid.
  • We will not text or call you unless you have clearly agreed to that.

The legal basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time, and it's quick: reply "unsubscribe" to any email.

Where it's stored and who sees it

Your details are kept in a private Google Sheet and our emails are sent through Google (Gmail), so Google processes the data on our behalf. We never sell or share your details with anyone else.

Visitor stats

We count page visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly tool. It uses no cookies and doesn't identify you. It only records things like which page was viewed, the referring site (for example Instagram) and your rough country.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If we haven't been in touch for 2 years, we delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Reply to any of our emails and we'll do it within one month. If you're unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
